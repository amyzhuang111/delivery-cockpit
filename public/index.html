How to use

  1. Open the page. It loads two illustrative CSVs (synthetic, seeded) so every panel has something to show.
  2. Set the project target and deadline in Daily rate. The red/green tile says whether the current pace gets there.
  3. Upload your task-review export in Acceptance. Columns needed: who did it, outcome (accepted/approved/pass vs rejected/fail/rework), and optionally team, vendor, reviewer, date. Names don't need to match exactly.
  4. Upload your contributor export in Contributor ramp: one row per person, a date column per stage. Blank = didn't reach it. Check the "matched columns" line under the upload.
  5. Enter N, the date, and how many people you can recruit per day. The verdict says yes/no, what's required per day, and the earliest date that works.
  6. Log any change you make in the Decision log so the reason is on record.

Sample files: illustrative-ramp.csv · illustrative-tasks.csv (download one, rename the headers, upload it back to see the matching work).

CaveatBoth are academic crowdsourcing pipelines for hard NLP tasks with strict filters. They are a pessimistic anchor, not a benchmark for expert-network onboarding. The "1,000 contributors in 3 days" scenario is the example given in the role description, not a measured rate.
